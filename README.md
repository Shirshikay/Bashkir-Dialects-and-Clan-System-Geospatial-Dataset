# Bashkir-Dialects-and-Clan-System-Geospatial-Dataset
Unique geospatial dataset covering village coordinates, dialect groups, sub-dialects, and historical clan across Bashkortostan and neighboring regions.


* **Code & Data** (`.geojson`, `.csv`, `.shp`, `.xlsx`): 
  The project is distributed under the **Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0)** license.

  
* **Geographical Data Sources**: 
  Base geospatial features (rivers, regional administrative boundaries) are extracted from **OpenStreetMap** (licensed under ODbL).


* **Linguistic & Ethnographic Data**:
  Village coordinates, dialect/sub-dialect mapping, and clan borders were independently aggregated and verified by the author. The dataset is built upon geographic descriptions from the "Kitap" state publishing house and the works of Rashit Shakur (Shakurov R. Z.), which are deeply rooted in the classic dialectological research of N. Kh. Maksyutova and S. F. Mirzhanova. It also incorporates ethnographic data from the "Bashkir Clans" multi-volume project by S. I. Khamidullin and the State Assembly "Kurultay" publishing house.
